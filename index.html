🧠 How the AI Works

1
NLP Preprocessing

Text is normalized and tokenized using Natural Language Processing.

2
Feature Extraction

URLs, urgency signals (CAPS, !!!), and suspicious patterns are extracted.

3
Weighted Keyword Scoring

Keywords are matched and assigned risk weights (HIGH=3, MED=2, LOW=1).

4
Multi-Class Classification

The scam type is identified: Phishing, Lottery, Financial, Job Scam, etc.

5
Sigmoid Confidence Score

A sigmoid function converts the raw score into a 0–100% confidence rating.

6
Explainable AI (XAI)

Every decision is explained in plain language so you understand why it was flagged.
